[Français](posts/009-esconder-bitcoin-ainda-protege/fr.md) ·
[Italiano](posts/009-esconder-bitcoin-ainda-protege/it.md)

Hiding is the standard advice for anyone afraid of being kidnapped over their
bitcoin, and all three parts of it are bets on what the criminal will believe.
Brazil records nine kidnappings for every armed robbery, on twelve incidents of
a press-sourced sample, so read that line as directional.

*The editions here have been revised since publication: the four audit questions
were rewritten and the contact filled in. The article as it ran is unchanged.*

The standard advice on not getting robbed for your bitcoin was good advice for a
world that stopped existing. Four conditions used to make holding safe; the one
that mattered was that nobody could tell which person held.
